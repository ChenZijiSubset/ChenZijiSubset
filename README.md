## 👋 Hi there, I'm Ziji Chen (陈籽吉)

I am a DPhil researcher in Engineering Science at the **University of Oxford**, supervised by [Prof. Noa Zilberman](https://eng.ox.ac.uk/people/noa-zilberman). My research focuses on **AI infrastructure and machine learning systems**, particularly ML workload characterisation, performance bottleneck identification, and non-intrusive infrastructure observability.

Previously, I obtained an MSc in Applied Computational Science and Engineering from Imperial College London and a BSc in Atmospheric Science, with a Minor in Accounting, from Sun Yat-sen University. I also worked as an infrastructure engineer at Tencent Cloud.
